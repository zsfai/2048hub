Yangwang U9X — The Record Behind the Top Tile

On 1 November 2026 in Abu Dhabi, BYD’s luxury brand Yangwang drove the U9 Xtreme (U9X) to 503.17 km/h — the first production car recognised past the 500 km/h mark in a single-direction run. The Fédération Internationale de l’Automobile (FIA) recognised the result, and Guinness listed one-way records for fastest production car and fastest electric production car.
