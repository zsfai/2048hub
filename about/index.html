How to win in 2048

Winning 2048 takes strategy and practice. Essentials:

  • Master the corner strategy: Keep your highest tile in one corner and build around it.
  • Build in one direction: Choose left, right, or down as your primary direction and stick to it.
  • Create cascading merges: Plan moves that trigger multiple merges in sequence.
  • Control the board: Keep rows and columns organized instead of scattering tiles.
  • Practice regularly: Pattern recognition improves with every run.

Origin of the 2048 game

The 2048 puzzle has a short but viral history:

  • Created by Gabriele Cirulli: The original 2048 was built by Italian web developer Gabriele Cirulli in March 2014.
  • Inspired by Threes!: The design was inspired by the mobile game Threes! by Asher Vollmer.
  • Open source: Cirulli released 2048 as open source, which led to countless themes and variants.
  • Viral success: Millions of players discovered it within days of launch.
  • Educational use: Teachers often use 2048 to practice mental math and planning.
